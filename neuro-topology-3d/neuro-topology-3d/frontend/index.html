Abstract

Understanding the inner circuitry of foundation models remains a grand challenge in AI alignment. While Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs) successfully decompose polysemantic residual representations into monosemantic feature dictionaries, the higher-order geometric interactions among millions of latent features have resisted real-time analysis. We propose NeuroTopology 3D, an open-source framework mapping 16,384 SAE feature directions into a 3D Riemannian Poincaré manifold equipped with real-time persistent homology filtration. We introduce Topological Circuit Coherence (TCC), a metric quantifying the harmonic loop persistence of reasoning subgraphs. Across 1,200 empirical trials on HarmBench and AdvGLUE, we demonstrate that adversarial jailbreak vectors trigger an immediate topological shatter ($\Delta H_{\text{topo}} > 1.45, p < 0.001$), providing an algorithmic foundation for real-time latent space anomaly detection.
